1. The Two-Terminal MOS Structure & Capacitor Operation

The MOS capacitor consists of a metal (or polysilicon) gate, silicon dioxide insulator (), and semiconductor substrate. Based on gate bias relative to substrate:

  • Accumulation ( on p-sub): Holes accumulate at the oxide-semiconductor interface.
  • Depletion ( but ): Gate repels holes, leaving fixed negatively charged ionized acceptor atoms () creating a depletion region of depth .
  • Inversion (): Surface potential reaches , bending bands sufficiently to attract a dense sheet of minority conduction electrons forming the inversion channel.

2. N-MOSFET Current-Voltage Regimes

When drain voltage is applied, current flows from drain to source through the induced channel. Three distinct regions govern operation:

  • Cutoff (): Channel is absent; .
  • Triode/Linear ( and ): Continuous channel from source to drain acts as a voltage-controlled linear resistor.
  • Saturation/Pinch-Off (): Channel depth at drain end reduces to zero (pinch-off). Drain current saturates and becomes independent of (ideally).
