1. The Four Feedback Topologies

Negative feedback samples a portion of the output signal and feeds it back out of phase to the input. The four classifications depend on sampling (voltage or current) and mixing (series or shunt):

  • Voltage-Series (Series-Shunt): Samples voltage (), mixes voltage in series (). Increases input resistance (), decreases output resistance (). Ideal voltage amplifier.
  • Voltage-Shunt (Shunt-Shunt): Samples voltage, mixes current in shunt. Decreases both and . Transresistance amplifier.
  • Current-Series (Series-Series): Samples current, mixes voltage in series. Increases both and . Transconductance amplifier.
  • Current-Shunt (Shunt-Series): Samples current, mixes current in shunt. Decreases , increases . Current amplifier.
