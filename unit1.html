1. Diode Clippers (Limiters) & Clampers (DC Restorers)

Clipping circuits shape input AC waveforms by removing portions of the signal above or below predetermined reference levels using diodes and DC reference biases. Clampers, or DC restorers, shift the baseline of the signal without altering its peak-to-peak amplitude by utilizing a capacitor and diode with a time constant .

2. BJT Physical Transport & Carrier Injection

In an NPN bipolar transistor biased in the active region, the forward-biased Base-Emitter junction injects excess minority electrons from the heavily doped emitter into the narrow base. These electrons diffuse across the base, where less than 1% recombine with majority holes, and are swept across the reverse-biased Collector-Base depletion region by the strong electric field.
